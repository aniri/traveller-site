Table of Contents

How to Travel the World on $50 a Day: Travel Cheaper, Longer, Smarter by Matt Kepnes

“Whether you’re a savvy backpacker or just dreaming of getting a passport and going overseas, Matt’s collection of easy-to-employ money-saving strategies will open your eyes to the near-infinite ways of seeing the world without busting your budget.” ~ Matt Gross, former New York Times Frugal Traveler

Really useful travel hacking tips, from discount travel cards, to buying cheap plane flights, finding the best deals and earning frequent flyer miles. Matt’s travel blog is also full of tips and useful info.
